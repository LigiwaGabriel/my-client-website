About Kilnfolk

A studio built around one kiln, two wheels, and a lot of shared clay-covered aprons.

How the studio started

Kilnfolk opened in a converted garage in 2021, after its founder ran out of shelf space for pots at home. What began as a single wheel and a borrowed kiln has grown into a studio that now teaches around thirty students a term.

What we believe about learning pottery

Clay is forgiving. Almost everything can be reworked, re-wedged, or recycled, and that changes how people learn: slowly, by hand, with room to fail without much cost.

Small classes

Courses are capped at eight students so that every potter gets real time at the wheel each session, not just a demonstration.

Shared equipment, shared care

Members clean and maintain the wheels and kiln together. It keeps costs low and, most terms, teaches people more about the craft than the throwing itself does.
