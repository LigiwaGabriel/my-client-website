Clay, taught patiently, in the heart of Kampala.

Kilnfolk is a small studio where beginners and returning potters share wheels, glazes, and one very hardworking kiln.

See our current classes and open studio hours

What happens at the studio

Wheel-throwing

Guided sessions on the pottery wheel, from centring your first lump of clay to trimming a finished bowl.

Handbuilding

Coil, slab, and pinch techniques for mugs, planters, and small sculptural pieces — no wheel required.

Open studio

Members can book unstructured studio time to work on personal projects using our wheels and glazes.

From the studio shelf

New to pottery? The Introduction to Wheel-Throwing course on the Classes page is the usual starting point — no experience needed.
